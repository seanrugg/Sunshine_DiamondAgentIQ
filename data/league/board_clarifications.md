# Board clarifications to the house rules

`house_rules_source.txt` is kept exactly as the league published it. Where the board has clarified or corrected it, the change is recorded here and applied in `house_rules.csv`, `pitch_rest.csv` and `league.json`.

| Date | Rule | Clarification |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-24 | 18U pitch count (H016) | 25 pitches or fewer needs no rest day. (The document says "fewer than 25" and "26–50".) |
| 2026-10-24 | Warm-up pitches (H027) | 13U uses the same warm-up rule as 10U and 18U: 5 warm-up pitches, 3 if the pitcher has already pitched more than 2 innings. |
| 2026-10-24 | 18U field (H006, league.json) | "Walker-Grant Middle School Armstrong Field" is the former name. The field is the James Monroe High School baseball field at Gladys-West Elementary School in Fredericksburg, VA (the "JMHS stadium" in the 18U heading). |
