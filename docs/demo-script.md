# 3-minute demo

1. **The problem (20 s).** Sunshine's website handles registration. Nobody teaches the parents, new coaches and teen umpires the rules, so disputes start in the stands. The mission is life lessons for every kid, including kids facing challenges.
2. **Install (20 s).** Open the app on a phone, Add to Home Screen, turn on airplane mode. It still works.

   Before the demo: on the demo phone, download the phone model over Wi-Fi (header pill → AI on this phone) and ask one question so it's warmed up. On an older phone, show the setup card recommending Offline.
3. **The teachable moment (45 s).** Parent · "My son struck out looking to end the game. How do I help him?" → the moment reframed (information, not identity), the lesson *A game built on failure*, what it means for the parent first, then for the player and coach, one thing to try this week, beyond the diamond, and who to talk to (let him talk first on the ride home). This is the difference: a stressful moment becomes a lesson for the whole family.
   Then Parent · "Can I tell the umpire he missed a call?" → *Umpire's judgment*, Sunshine's rule that only the head coach discusses calls, and the lesson *Control the moment*.
4. **Coach (30 s).** Coach · 13U · "He threw 70 pitches today. When can he pitch again?" → 4 calendar days under the Sunshine 13U rule, computed in code. Show the Pitch Count tab with the exact date.
5. **House rules (15 s).** Parent · 10U · "Can my kid wear cleats?" → no, sneakers or turf shoes on the Sunshine turf. Switch to 18U → cleats allowed at the James Monroe High School field. Same question, the league's own answer for each division.
6. **Player (20 s).** Player · "I made the last out. How do I get past it?" → coaching moment, one thing to practise.
7. **Answer options (40 s).** Tap the header pill. Show the four options: *Offline* for any phone, *AI on this phone* (already downloaded on the demo phone; airplane mode still on, ask "Can I slide headfirst in 18U?" and it answers on the device), *My AI provider* (Google AI Studio key, Load models, Test), *League server*. Same rules underneath every one; the AI only explains.
8. **The pitch to leagues (20 s).** Swap `data/league/` (house rules, pitch tables, league info) and any rec league gets its own edition on its own rulebook. Nothing is invented: every answer cites the league's document or a rulebook source.
